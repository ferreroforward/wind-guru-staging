⚠️ Use at your own risk

Wind Guru is a free, unofficial forecast tool built by a local rider. Its forecasts, odds, spot notes and recommendations can be wrong, out of date or incomplete, and conditions on the water change fast.

Wind and water sports are dangerous. Using this site, and acting on anything it shows, is entirely at your own risk. You are responsible for checking official forecasts and live conditions, knowing and following local rules and regulations, judging your own ability and equipment, and deciding whether it is safe to go out.

By using this site you accept all risk and all liability for your use of its information and recommendations, and you agree that Wind Guru and its creator are not liable for any injury, loss or damage.

Wind Guru AI, the chat on this page, is an AI and can be wrong. Questions, answers and any feedback sent through it are kept for up to 90 days, without names or IP addresses, to improve it. A conditions report is only sent if you tap Send, and like all reports it becomes public on GitHub.

How this works. We check the wind forecast for every spot and show your odds of getting your chosen wind speed, hour by hour. Green means good odds, yellow means maybe, red means unlikely.

Tuned by local knowledge. Some spots have quirky local wind patterns that generic forecasts miss — we adjust for that using real reports from local riders and nearby live wind stations, so the numbers get more accurate over time. Tap any hour on a spot’s forecast and use "Report actual conditions" to help tune it further.

Learned from a year of station data. For Steveston, Tsawwassen, Erwin Park and Porteau (through Pam Rocks), the number comes from a blend of the best models fitted to a full year of Environment Canada station readings, which fixes each model's local habits. When it says 70%, it has happened about 70% of the time. It is rescored every night; see How we're doing.

Only the good options are listed below. The card at the top always calls out the single windiest good-conditions hour across every spot, no matter which quick pick is selected; the lists below only include spots with a decent shot at your chosen range, to keep the page short — every spot is still shown on the map above regardless of odds.
